Before you come

The first consultation is more useful when the history is in front of the doctor.

  1. Bring your reports. Any X-rays, MRI films, ultrasound reports or clinic notes, even if they are old.

  2. Bring your medicine list. A written list, or the boxes themselves, is enough.

  3. Note when it hurts. Morning, after sitting, on stairs, at night. That pattern guides the examination more than the pain score.

  4. Allow time. The clinic runs for three hours, so arrive within the session rather than at the end of it.

If your pain is severe, or you cannot bear weight after an injury, do not wait for the weekly clinic. Seek urgent care first.
