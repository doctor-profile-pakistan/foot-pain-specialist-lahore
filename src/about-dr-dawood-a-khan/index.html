Dr. Dawood A. Khan is an interventional pain and spine physician in Lahore. He treats persistent pain without surgery, using image-guided procedures and regenerative medicine.

Profile

Training in anaesthesiology, followed by a fellowship in interventional pain medicine.

Dr. Khan trained in anaesthesiology before specialising in interventional pain medicine. His anaesthesia background underpins the safety and sedation aspects of his procedural work, while his pain fellowship focuses on identifying the specific structure responsible for a patient's pain.

His practice covers spinal and musculoskeletal pain, nerve-related pain, and joint and soft tissue procedures. Procedures are performed under ultrasound or C-Arm guidance. Alongside these, he offers regenerative therapies including platelet-rich plasma, growth factor therapy and exosome therapy.

He is Chief Consultant Anaesthetist at LGH and a consultant in regenerative medicine.

Areas of practice

Conditions assessed and treated at the clinic.

  • Foot and ankle pain → Plantar fasciitis, heel pain, Achilles tendinopathy, ankle pain
  • Joint and musculoskeletal pain → Ultrasound and C-Arm guided joint injections
  • Nerve and spine pain → Diagnostic and therapeutic nerve blocks
  • Regenerative medicine → Platelet-rich plasma, growth factor and exosome therapy

Questions about the practice

For anything not covered here, call the clinic.

What does an interventional pain physician do?

An interventional pain physician treats pain using minimally invasive procedures rather than medication alone. This includes image-guided injections, nerve blocks and regenerative therapies, with the aim of treating the structure that is generating the pain.

Does Dr. Khan perform surgery?

No. All procedures offered are minimally invasive and performed without surgery. They are carried out on an outpatient basis under ultrasound or C-Arm guidance.

What should I bring to the first appointment?

Bring any previous X-rays, MRI films, ultrasound reports or clinical notes, along with a list of medicines you currently take. If you have had an injection or procedure elsewhere, bring that record as well.
